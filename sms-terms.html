SMS Terms

Last updated: August 2026
We only text you if you ticked a box asking us to. You are never required to accept texts in order to place an order, and you can stop them at any time by replying STOP.

The Two Kinds of Message

They are separate. Agreeing to one does not sign you up for the other, and you can stop either without affecting the other.

  • Order messages. A confirmation when you place an order, a reminder if we have not received payment, and a notice with tracking when it ships. Only ever about your own order.
  • Deals and new products. Occasional marketing messages. A few a month at most.

How Often

Order messages depend on you — typically one to three per order. Marketing messages are a few per month at most.

What It Costs

We do not charge for text messages. Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier, depending on your plan.

Stopping Messages

Reply STOP to any message and we will stop texting you. It takes effect immediately and you will get one confirmation message.

Reply HELP to any message and we will reply with how to reach us.

You can also email us and ask to be removed. If you change your mind, reply START or tick the box again at checkout.

What We Do With Your Number

We use it to send the messages described above and to contact you about an order. We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number with anyone for marketing. Your consent is stored with the date and the exact wording you agreed to, so there is a record of what you signed up for.

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle the rest of your information.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to your carrier's network and your device.

Age Requirement

You must be 21 or older to order from us or to receive our messages.

Changes

If we change these terms, the updated version appears here with a new date. The wording you agreed to when you opted in is what we hold on record for you.

Contact Us
