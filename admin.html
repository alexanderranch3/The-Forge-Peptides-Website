Why this is separate

Labels are a real per-vial cost your supplier never charges — Direct Peptides ships unlabeled and the labels come from a printer you pay separately. Keeping them here means switching providers is one number, not re-entering every order.

🔒 Changing the rate never rewrites an order you've already written. Each purchase order keeps the rate it was created with, so last month's landed cost stays what you actually paid. A new rate applies to new orders.
